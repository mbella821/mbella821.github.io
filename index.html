About Me

Research Philosophy & Background

Bridging theoretical signal processing with real-world scientific challenges across astrophysics, gravitational waves, and audio.

From Signals to Science

My research is driven by a single, powerful question: how do we extract meaningful information when the data is buried under overwhelming noise and interference? This challenge appears everywhere - from the faint whispers of gravitational waves detected by space-based interferometers, to the contaminated spectra of distant galaxies, to overlapping voices in a crowded room.

I specialize in developing robust source separation and recovery algorithms that combine the rigor of model-based signal processing with the representational power of deep learning. My hybrid approaches respect the underlying physics while leveraging data-driven insights to push beyond classical limits.

Currently at IRAP in Toulouse, France, I contribute to two of ESA's most ambitious space missions - Euclid and LISA - while maintaining active research in audio and speech processing.

LISA - Gravitational-Wave Source Separation

Deep Learning · Time-Frequency · Weak Signal Detection · Inverse Problems

The LISA (Laser Interferometer Space Antenna) mission will be the first space-based gravitational-wave observatory. The data stream is a complex superposition of multiple GW sources - compact binary inspirals, extreme mass-ratio inspirals, and stochastic backgrounds - all buried under instrumental noise orders of magnitude stronger than the signals.

Neural Architecture
Research Objectives
Multi-source GW separation
Weak-signal detection in noisy env.
Time-frequency representation opt.
Deep NNs: U-Net, Residual blocks
Complex-valued multi-channel signals
Physics-aware hybrid approaches
Large-scale synthetic datasets
GPU-accelerated training
Methodology

I develop deep learning architectures incorporating physical priors about GW propagation and detector response. The approach combines time-frequency representations (spectrograms, wavelet transforms) with U-Net and residual architectures trained on massive synthetic datasets from realistic waveform models. The key innovation: preserving the physical structure of recovered signals while achieving robust separation at extremely low SNR.

Euclid - Spectral Decontamination & Reconstruction

Astrophysical Signal Processing · Hyperspectral · Inverse Problems · PSF-Aware

The ESA Euclid mission maps the geometry of the dark Universe. Its NISP instrument performs slitless spectroscopy: galaxy spectra are dispersed across the detector without physical slits, causing severe spectral contamination where neighboring galaxies' spectra overlap.

Convolutive Blind Source Separation

BSS · Audio · Speech · Underdetermined Mixtures · RTF-Based Recombination

Real-world acoustic environments produce convolutive mixtures where each source is filtered by the room's impulse response. When there are fewer microphones than sources (underdetermined case), classical methods fail. My research relaxes strict sparsity assumptions.

Three-Stage Separation Pipeline
Developed Approaches
Relaxed sparsity TF masking
Bayesian estimation frameworks
Blind RTF estimation
RTF-based recombination
Beamforming integration
EM clustering optimization

Convolutive-BSS-Speech-Separation
MATLAB

MATLAB implementation of the method from Computer Speech & Language, 2026. Clean, modular, handles both determined and underdetermined convolutive mixtures.

BSS_RTF_Recombination/
  ├── main.m % Entry point
  ├── src/
  │ ├── compute_stft.m % STFT analysis
  │ ├── compute_istft.m % ISTFT synthesis
  │ ├── extract_complex_cosine_features.m % Features
  │ ├── cluster_em_frequency_bins.m % EM clustering
  │ ├── estimate_rtf.m % Blind RTF estimation
  │ ├── reconstruct_sources.m % Recombination
  │ └── evaluate_separation.m % Evaluation
  └── utils/ % EM helpers

Configurable via params: window=2048, FFT=2048, overlap=512, EM max=100, tol=1e-7, RTF thresh=0.95.
